## **Project Overview**

The **E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard** provides a three-part view of an e-commerce business, covering **customer acquisition and sales performance, customer experience, and demand and inventory availability**.

The goal was to move beyond sales figures and understand **who the customers are, how they are acquired, whether they continue to contribute over time, how well orders are delivered, and where inventory constraints may be causing lost sales**.

The dashboard consists of three main pages:

- **Customer & Sales Overview** – Customer acquisition, segments, acquisition channels, categories, orders, and sales performance.
- **Customer Experience** – Contribution margin, delivery performance, returns, customer cohorts, and ordering behavior.
- **Demand & Availability** – Ordered versus fulfilled quantities, fulfillment rate, unfulfilled demand, lost sales, stock status, and fulfillment models.

At the overall level, the business has 2,200 customers generating 5,873 orders, with €789.19K in gross sales, €640.42K in net sales, and an AOV of 109.05.

## **Problem Statement**

The business needs a clearer understanding of its **customer acquisition, sales performance, customer experience, and inventory availability**.

Looking at sales alone does not explain:

- Which customer segments contribute most to the business.
- Which acquisition channels attract the most customers.
- Whether newly acquired customers continue to generate value over time.
- How customer ordering behavior changes as they mature.
- Whether orders are delivered within the promised timeframe.
- Which product categories experience higher return rates.
- How fulfillment models differ in delivery and return outcomes.
- Where inventory shortages are preventing orders from being fulfilled.
- How much potential sales value may be lost due to unfulfilled demand.

The dashboard brings these areas together to provide a more complete view of **commercial performance, customer experience, and operational demand fulfillment**.

##  **Key Insights**

- **Customer acquisition is primarily driven by Paid Social and Organic Search**, while New and Growth customers represent a significant share of the customer base.
- **Customer ordering activity declines as customers mature**, highlighting a potential retention and engagement gap beyond the initial purchase.
- **Customer experience has two notable pressure points:** overall on-time delivery is **92.75%**, while the overall return rate is **10.04%**. Fashion stands out with a significantly higher **23% return rate**.
- **Fulfillment performance is strong at 95.65%**, but **498 units remain unfulfilled**, representing **€36.71K in lost sales value**.
- **Electronics has the highest unfulfilled quantity and lost sales value**, making it the most significant inventory-related concern.
- **Backorder/Unavailable inventory accounts for the largest share of unfulfilled demand**, indicating a need for stronger inventory availability management.
- **Fulfillment models perform differently across delivery speed, on-time delivery, and return rates**, showing that operational performance should be assessed using multiple service metrics.



##  **Business Recommendations**

- **Strengthen customer retention strategies** to encourage New and Growth customers to continue ordering throughout the customer lifecycle.
- **Investigate the 23% Fashion return rate** by identifying the products and return reasons contributing most to returns.
- **Prioritize inventory planning for Electronics**, particularly products with high unfulfilled demand and lost sales value.
- **Reduce Backorder/Unavailable inventory** through improved demand forecasting, replenishment planning, and stock monitoring.
- **Evaluate fulfillment models using multiple performance indicators**, including delivery time, on-time delivery, and return rate.
- **Assess acquisition channels based on customer value, not just customer volume**, by connecting acquisition sources to revenue, contribution, repeat purchases, and retention.
